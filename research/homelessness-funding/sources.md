## CCO mechanisms

- **share-guidance** — Oregon Health Authority, "2026 SHARE Guidance." Formula under OAR 410-141-3735: greater of 0%–20% of three-year average adjusted net income on a sliding scale by risk-based capital (0% at or below 300% RBC to 20% at or above 500% RBC), or 10% of dividends; housing-related services and supports is the statewide priority and a portion of each year's SHARE must go to it. https://www.oregon.gov/oha/HPA/dsi-tc/Documents/SHARE-Initiative-Guidance-Document.pdf
- **oha-housing-medicaid** — Oregon Health Authority Transformation Center, "CCO Guidance: Housing and Medicaid Spending Programs," updated December 2025. CCOs invested over $104M in housing through SHARE and flexible services 2018–2024; SHARE may fund capital, flexible services may not; HRSN must be used first where the member is eligible; SHARE has funded five Project Turnkey sites and FUSE in four communities. https://www.oregon.gov/oha/HPA/dsi-tc/Documents/Housing-and-Medicaid-Spending-Programs.pdf
- **careoregon-housing** — CareOregon, "Housing is health: Investing in solutions." $3M to Washington County permanent supportive housing; $2M OnTrack Rogue Valley; smaller eviction-prevention and shelter grants. https://www.careoregon.org/mental-health/stories/housing-is-health-investing-in-solutions

## Evidence from elsewhere

- **rand-h4h** — RAND, "Evaluation of Housing for Health Permanent Supportive Housing Program," RR-1694 (2017). County costs $38,146 before to $15,358 after; housing $15,288; $1.20 saved per $1; ED visits −68%, inpatient −77%; DHS spending down about $20M. https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RR1694.html
- **providence-core** — Providence Center for Outcomes Research and Education, "Housing is Health" and "CORE study shows benefits of major local housing for health initiative." $21.5M from six health systems to Central City Concern (2016); 1,600 Medicaid members, 145 properties; ED −18%, inpatient −15%, primary care +20%, Medicaid spend −12%. https://blog.providence.org/center-for-outcomes-research-education/core-study-shows-benefits-of-major-local-housing-for-health-initiative
- **urban-denver** — Urban Institute, "Denver Supportive Housing Social Impact Bond Initiative: Final Outcome Payments" (2021). $8.6M invested; 250 people; 86% housed at one year, 81% at two; $6,876 lower annual service cost vs. $25,554 control; $9.6M repaid. https://www.urban.org/research/publication/denver-supportive-housing-social-impact-bond-initiative-final-outcome-payments
- **nc-hop** — NCDHHS, "Healthy Opportunities Pilots Lead to Healthier Outcomes and Reduce NC Medicaid Costs," June 2, 2026. −$164 per member per month; 31,000+ people; UNC Sheps Center; program suspended after the General Assembly declined funding past July 1, 2025 despite CMS authorization through December 2029. https://www.ncdhhs.gov/news/press-releases/2026/06/02/healthy-opportunities-pilots-lead-healthier-outcomes-and-reduce-nc-medicaid-costs
- **mn-hss** — Minnesota DHS, "Housing Stabilization Services program ends Oct. 31" and CMS SPA MN-25-0021. First-in-nation 1915(i) housing benefit terminated October 31, 2025 after rapid provider and spending growth drew state and federal investigation. https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/MNDHS/bulletins/3f9856a
- **wa-fcs** — Washington State DSHS, "The Foundational Community Supports Program: Preliminary Evaluation Findings," and HCA program page. Launched January 2018; 20,000+ enrolled; supportive housing associated with reduced ED and inpatient use. https://www.hca.wa.gov/about-hca/programs-and-initiatives/medicaid-transformation-project-mtp/foundational-community-supports
- **calaim** — California DHCS, Section 1115 waivers page and CalAIM renewal materials. Renewal filed May 12, 2026; transitional rent mandatory for plans January 1, 2026. https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/waivers/
